Questions, answered

The essentials about charts, numbers and tarot.

What is a natal (birth) chart and how is it calculated?+
A natal chart is a map of the sky at your exact birth moment and place. Horoscope computes planet positions with the VSOP87 astronomical model and derives your Ascendant and houses from your birth time and coordinates.
How accurate are these calculators?+
The astronomy is professional-grade (VSOP87) and runs entirely in your browser. Interpretations are general, symbolic and for entertainment and self-reflection only.
Is my birth information kept private?+
Yes. All calculations run locally in your browser, so your birth date, time and place are never sent to a server.
