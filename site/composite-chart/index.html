Composite Chart: What It Shows and How the Midpoints Work

VERDICT

A composite chart combines corresponding positions from two natal charts into a symbolic relationship chart, usually using midpoints. It describes the relationship within an astrological framework; it does not measure compatibility or prove that two people should stay together. Check the calculation method and birth-time reliability before interpreting houses.

What am I actually looking at?

You are looking at a new set of calculated positions, not either person's natal sky. A composite Sun comes from the two Sun positions; a composite Moon comes from the two Moon positions. The resulting set need not describe a sky that existed at a real moment. This is why a composite should be labelled with its method rather than treated as another person's birth chart.

Synastry keeps the two natal charts separate and compares their positions. A midpoint composite creates a third set. Ask which question you want to explore: how two individuals interact, or how you describe the relationship as a shared pattern. Neither format supplies evidence about consent, honesty or the quality of daily behaviour.

An original midpoint example you can check

These numbers are invented teaching inputs, not a real couple's chart. Put one Sun at 350 degrees and the other at 10 degrees of zodiac longitude. The shorter arc crosses zero Aries and is 20 degrees long. Its midpoint is zero degrees, not 180 degrees. Simply averaging the printed values would choose the opposite midpoint.

Write the calculation as a signed shortest difference: ((B - A + 540) modulo 360) - 180. Add half that difference to A and wrap the result into 0 through 360 degrees. For A = 350 and B = 10, the difference is 20 and the wrapped midpoint is zero. Reverse the two inputs: the difference is minus 20 and the midpoint is still zero.

Now try A = 40 and B = 100. The difference is 60 and the midpoint is 70, or 10 degrees Gemini. At A = 0 and B = 180, the two arcs are equally short. The possible midpoints are 90 and 270. That boundary needs an explicit convention; this teaching audit reports ambiguity instead of selecting a relationship meaning from one arbitrary choice.

Keep a four-line calculation record

First record the original pair, including which body each number describes. Second record the coordinate frame: do not mix a tropical position with a sidereal position. Third record the midpoint convention and the software's settings. Fourth record what is unresolved, including any uncertain birth time. This short record is more useful than a screenshot whose settings are hidden.

For the first example, your record reads: Sun A 350, Sun B 10; same zodiac frame; shorter-arc midpoint zero; houses not supplied. Nothing in that record identifies a house, an Ascendant or a successful relationship. Those would be extra claims, requiring extra inputs or evidence.

A tool may use different conventions for composite angles and houses. Astrodienst documents both midpoint and reference-place methods. Two outputs with different house settings are not necessarily calculation errors. Compare the settings and source data before comparing interpretations.

Read a pattern without turning it into a verdict on a person

Choose one verified placement and turn it into a question about a shared activity. Instead of announcing that a composite placement means perfect communication, ask how the two of you handle an unfinished disagreement. Each person can write an answer independently before comparing notes. Agreement with an interpretation is an observation, not proof of the chart's predictive power.

Use three columns for a two-week reflection: the proposed theme, one concrete interaction, and an alternative explanation. For example, a missed conversation may reflect mismatched work schedules. Keep that explanation beside any symbolic reading. If the examples repeatedly contradict the interpretation, change the interpretation rather than forcing the relationship to fit it.

Do not transfer a composite description to one partner as an accusation. A shared chart does not establish which person caused a problem. Responsibility comes from actions and agreements, which a midpoint calculation cannot observe.

Questions before you calculate

Can I use it without both birth times? Some planetary positions may still be estimated, but their uncertainty must be carried into the result. The Moon and angles need particular care. Do not present guessed composite houses as confirmed. A missing input is a reason to withhold the affected conclusion.

Does switching partner A and B change the result? For the shorter-arc planetary midpoint demonstrated above, swapping the order preserves the result except that an exactly opposite pair needs an explicit tie convention. If a tool changes other outputs, check reference location, house method and input settings.

Does HoroscopeTools calculate this chart here? This page is a guide and worked example, not a complete composite calculator. The natal-chart tool helps you inspect an individual chart. It does not combine two charts into a composite.

Reject the claim that a good composite guarantees a good relationship

A calculated midpoint cannot establish whether somebody respects a boundary or keeps a promise. Use astrology, if useful to you, as a reflective language. Keep decisions grounded in actual behaviour and shared preferences. An attractive chart is not a reason to dismiss mistreatment.
