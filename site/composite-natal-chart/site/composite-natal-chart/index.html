THE CALCULATION

Midpoints, stated plainly.

For each listed body, this calculator computes tropical geocentric longitude for both birth moments with a locally bundled Swiss Ephemeris model. It then takes the shortest-arc midpoint between those two longitudes. The result is a midpoint composite chart, not a Davison chart.

Dates from 1 January 1800 through 31 December 2399 are accepted. Birth time and UTC offset are required because the Moon can move substantially within a day. The tool does not calculate houses: those require a chosen place and house system, and this page deliberately does not infer either.

The bundled model runs locally in your browser. It does not send birth details to a server, use a preset date table, or query a city database.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Use the result well.

What is a composite natal chart?

It is a symbolic relationship chart built from the midpoints between two natal-chart longitudes. This calculator shows the planetary midpoint layer and major aspects among those midpoint positions.

Is this a Davison chart calculator?

No. A Davison chart uses a midpoint in time and location. This page uses the midpoint composite method and states that distinction before you calculate.

Why might another composite chart differ?

Check both recorded birth times, the UTC offsets in force on the dates, the zodiac type, and whether the other page uses midpoint composite or Davison methodology.

Where can I calculate an individual chart?

Open the natal chart calculator for an individual chart before comparing two records.
