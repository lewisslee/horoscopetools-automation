NATAL HOUSES · SEVENTH HOUSE

The 7th house is where a chart describes relationships that require an answer from someone else.

In astrology, the 7th house is commonly read through negotiated one-to-one bonds: partners, clients, collaborators, competitors and open opponents. It does not promise marriage or prove dependency. The useful question is how agreement, conflict and mutual obligation are actually handled.

Last updated November 15, 2026

Read the exchange, not a wedding forecast

The seventh house is sometimes reduced to a future spouse. That shortcut misses its basic shape: it sits opposite the first house, where “I” meets a person with their own terms, leverage and right to disagree. A contract, a close friendship, an adviser relationship, a business partnership or a public dispute can all make the house relevant.

Start with the sign on the seventh-house cusp, then look at its ruler: which sign and house is the ruler in, and what aspects does it make? Planets inside the seventh add functions to the encounter, but they do not erase the rest of the chart or the facts of a particular relationship.

Request

What can each person directly ask for? Make the request concrete enough to answer.

Response

What can each person accept, refuse or renegotiate without punishment?

Responsibility

What agreement is observable, and what consequence follows if it changes?

Use a reciprocity check before interpreting a placement

This Request / Response / Responsibility check is this page’s practical contribution. It keeps the seventh house tied to reciprocal contact rather than treating any longing for partnership as astrology’s proof.

Try it with one live arrangement. If you cannot identify a request, response and responsibility on both sides, pause the interpretation and clarify the relationship first. A chart can be a reflection prompt; it cannot supply consent, commitment or accountability.

A three-question chart note

  1. Record the house system your chart uses and the sign on the seventh-house cusp.
  2. Record the cusp ruler, its sign and its house.
  3. Write one current relationship situation where the three reciprocity questions can be observed.

Keep the note separate from a prediction. It records the calculation and a real-world context without claiming one causes the other.

What the seventh house does not establish

It does not establish who you will marry, whether a relationship is healthy, whether a person is faithful, or whether reconciliation is advisable. Those are decisions about behavior, safety and consent—not a house placement.

7th house questions

Is the 7th house only about romantic relationships?

No. It can also describe contractual, professional, adversarial and other reciprocal one-to-one relationships.

Does an empty 7th house mean I will be alone?

No. An empty house is not a verdict; read its cusp, ruler and the rest of the chart.

Do I need an exact birth time to read the 7th house?

Yes, a reliable birth time matters because house cusps can change when the recorded time changes.
