Sun and Moon sign calculator:
what each result needs.

A Sun and Moon sign calculator can show two different chart coordinates from the same birth record. Check the local calendar date for the Sun; check date, local time and birth place for the Moon—especially when the recorded moment is near a boundary.

Last updated November 2, 2026

Do not use one input rule for both

Sun result

Start with the recorded local date. If the record sits near a sign change, preserve its time-zone context rather than converting it casually from memory.

Moon result

Use the date, local time and birth city together. A rounded time should remain labelled as a rounded time when you compare results.

This page’s original contribution is the split-input receipt: preserve the separate check for each displayed label rather than treating “Sun and Moon” as one undifferentiated result.

Make a two-result receipt

Sun line: local date, displayed Sun sign, calculator setting.

Moon line: local date, recorded or estimated time, birth city, displayed Moon sign, calculator setting.

Boundary note: if a record is approximate, keep that uncertainty next to the time-sensitive Moon result instead of smoothing it out in the interpretation.

What these labels do not decide

A calculator can display selected coordinates under its visible settings. It cannot establish a fixed personality, predict an event or resolve a decision for you. Read a chart result as an interpretive prompt only after the input record is clear.

Use the star-chart reading order to validate the record before interpretation, or the find-my-birth-chart checklist if you are still locating it.

Sun and Moon sign calculator questions

Can I calculate my Sun and Moon sign without a birth time?

You can check a date-based result, but keep any time-sensitive uncertainty visible rather than treating an assumed time as evidence.

Why does the Moon result differ between calculators?

Compare the date, time, city and visible settings before comparing interpretation text.

Do I use my current city?

No. Use the birth city that belongs with the recorded birth moment.

Is the Moon sign the same as a Rising sign?

No. They are separate chart coordinates with different input dependencies; the Rising sign is especially sensitive to time and local place.
