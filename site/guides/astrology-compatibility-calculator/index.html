Astrology compatibility calculator:
ask one question.

An astrology compatibility calculator compares selected information from two chart records; it does not calculate whether a relationship will work. Preserve each record separately, name one comparison question, and distinguish an interpretive chart observation from a real-world choice.

Last updated November 3, 2026

A compatibility result needs three labels

Records

Save each person’s date, time, place and settings separately. Do not replace missing time with a confident house claim.

Question

Name one comparison question before reading the output, such as a selected pair of placements or a stated timing question.

Scope

Describe the calculation as an interpretive comparison, not evidence of consent, safety, commitment or personal worth.

This page’s original contribution is the three-label compatibility record. It separates source data, the question being asked and the scope of the answer so a generated score cannot silently stand in for all three.

Keep chart comparison and lived decisions apart

A relationship includes communication, consent, safety, circumstances and choice. None of those can be established by an astrology calculator. Use a chart comparison as a reflection prompt only after the records and question are clear.

For a two-chart workflow, see the synastry chart calculator guide. For a single base-chart reading, use the star-chart reading order.

When one birth time is unknown

Keep time-sensitive angles and houses unresolved for that person. You can record date-stable information, but do not let a default time manufacture precision in a comparison.

Astrology compatibility calculator questions

Can a compatibility calculator predict whether we will stay together?

No. It can display a selected chart comparison under stated settings, not make a reliable relationship forecast.

Do I need two exact birth times?

Exact times support time-sensitive comparisons. Without them, preserve the limitation and leave relevant angles and houses unresolved.

Is compatibility the same as a composite chart?

No. A composite chart uses a separate construction; a compatibility comparison should state which method it is using.

What should I save?

Save both input records, visible settings, the selected comparison and the exact question.
