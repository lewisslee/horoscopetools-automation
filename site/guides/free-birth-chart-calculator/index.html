Free birth chart calculator:
what it can confirm.

A free birth chart calculator can confirm the positions it calculates from your entered date, time, place and settings. It cannot confirm a house or Ascendant result when the birth time is unknown, approximate or entered under the wrong location.

Read the result by its input dependency

Date-led

Some placements can be checked from a date, but a chart still needs its displayed settings recorded before you compare results across services.

Time-led

The Ascendant and house pattern rely on local time. A default noon chart is a display convenience, not proof of an angle.

Place-led

A city establishes local coordinates and time-zone handling. Pick the actual birth place rather than a nearby city selected for convenience.

This page’s original contribution is the dependency read: label every line in a free result by the field it needs before interpreting it. That makes an uncertain time visible instead of silently converting it into a confident house claim.

A three-line result receipt

Line 1 — Source record: write the exact date, the recorded or estimated time, and the selected birth city.

Line 2 — Calculator settings: note the zodiac frame and house system displayed by the service.

Line 3 — Scope: mark angles and houses as unresolved if the time is unknown; keep only claims supported by the record.

Use the birth-chart input map before you generate a wheel. If you have a plausible time range, run the two-result Rising-sign check rather than accepting one time-sensitive label.

What “free” does not mean

Free does not mean a chart has no assumptions. It means the tool is available without a payment step; you still need to inspect its visible inputs, output labels and settings. A polished interpretation paragraph does not replace those checks.

For a single-chart reading order after generation, use the star chart calculator guide. To compare two separate records, use the synastry chart calculator guide. If you are still locating your record, follow the find-my-birth-chart checklist first.

Free birth chart calculator questions

Can I make a chart without a birth time?

Yes, but label the time-sensitive angles and houses unresolved instead of treating a placeholder time as verified.

Why do two free calculators disagree?

Compare the entered city, time zone, zodiac frame and house-system setting before comparing the interpretations.

Should I enter an approximate time?

You may use it as an estimate only when you preserve that label and test whether nearby times change the time-sensitive result.

Is a free chart enough to predict an event?

No. A chart result records a selected calculation; it is not a reliable predictor of events or a substitute for real-world decisions.
